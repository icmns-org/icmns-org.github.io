Hosted at McGill University, with contributed talks, poster sessions and minisymposia; selected posters were invited for oral presentations. The conference banquet on 4 June featured a joint lecture by Michael Mackey and Leon Glass, "Early Day Adventures in Mathematical Biology".

Supported by the Centre de recherches mathématiques (CRM), the Centre for Applied Mathematics in Bioscience and Medicine (CAMBAM), the Réseau de santé numérique (RSN), UNIQUE and the Department of Physiology at McGill University.
