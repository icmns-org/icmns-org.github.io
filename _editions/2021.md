The second digital edition, free of charge and streamed on YouTube. Contributed work was presented as one-slide micro-talks, and a panel discussion on the future of ICMNS was held.

## Plenary speakers
- Krešimir Josić (University of Houston, USA)
- Olivier Faugeras (Inria Sophia Antipolis Méditerranée, France)
- Kathrin Ohla (Helmut Schmidt University, Hamburg, Germany)

## Minisymposia
- Dynamic ion concentrations and neuronal network dynamics (Jonathan Rubin)
- Dynamical transitions in heterogeneous and structurally anisotropic neural networks (Kyle Wedgwood)
- Large scale neural network modeling with probabilistic spiking units (Christophe Pouzat)
- Mathematical models of synaptic plasticity (Olivier Faugeras)
- Mean-field models in neuroscience (Romain Veltz)
- Next-generation neural mass models: from spiking neurons to populations (Michael Forrester)
- Phase-amplitude reduction: Koopman and control (Zachary Kilpatrick)
- The mathematics of vision: from cortical-inspired modelling to perception (Luca Calatroni, Valentina Franceschi, Dario Prandi)
- Statistical and modeling problems in the study of spike trains (Laura Sacerdote)
