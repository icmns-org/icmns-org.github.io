The first edition held outside France. Topics included correlation analysis of network activity, information theory for plastic synapses, combinatorics for attractor neural networks, and data assimilation methods for neuroscience. Research and tutorials presented at the meeting were collected in a special issue of the *Journal of Mathematical Neuroscience*.
