The 12th International Conference on Mathematical Neuroscience will take place in Montpellier. The scientific programme will include keynote, invited and contributed talks.

For registration, abstract submission, venue and travel, see the **[official conference website](https://project.inria.fr/icmns2027/)**.

## Plenary speakers
- **Daniela Calvetti**
  Case Western Reserve University, USA
- **Daniela Egas Santander**
  Max Planck Institute of Molecular Cell Biology and Genetics, Dresden, Germany
- **Philippe Faure**
  ESPCI, CNRS, Paris, France
- **Alain Goriely**
  University of Oxford, UK
- **Alessandro Sarti**
  EHESS, CNRS, Paris, France
