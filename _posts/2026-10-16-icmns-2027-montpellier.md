title: ICMNS 2027 will take place in Montpellier
date: 2026-10-16 11:00:00 +0100
---
The 12th International Conference on Mathematical Neuroscience, ICMNS 2027, will take place in **Montpellier, France, on 1–4 June 2027**.

The plenary speakers are Daniela Calvetti (Case Western Reserve University), Daniela Egas Santander (Max Planck Institute of Molecular Cell Biology and Genetics, Dresden), Philippe Faure (ESPCI, CNRS, Paris), Alain Goriely (University of Oxford) and Alessandro Sarti (EHESS, CNRS, Paris).

The conference is organised by Emre Baspinar, Hugues Berry, Mathieu Desroches and Vivien Kirk. Key dates for abstract submission and registration will be announced on the [ICMNS 2027 page]({{ '/2027/' | relative_url }}), on the [official conference website](https://project.inria.fr/icmns2027/) and on the [mailing list]({{ site.mailing_list }}).
