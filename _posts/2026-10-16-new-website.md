---
title: A new home for ICMNS
---
ICMNS now has a permanent website at [icmns.org](https://icmns.org). Until now, each edition had its own site, hosted by its organisers; this site brings the series together in one place.

You will find here news about upcoming editions, an [archive of all past conferences]({{ '/editions/' | relative_url }}) since 2015, and information [about the series]({{ '/about/' | relative_url }}).

To receive announcements by email, subscribe to the [ICMNS mailing list](https://listserver.vu.nl/mailman/listinfo/icmns-beta).
