We are delighted to announce that the 13th International Conference on Mathematical Neuroscience, ICMNS 2028, will take place in Shanghai, China, hosted by **Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU)**.

## Co-chairs

- Songting Li (Shanghai Jiao Tong University)
- Douglas Zhou (Shanghai Jiao Tong University)

Dates, venue and further details will be announced here and on the [ICMNS mailing list](https://listserver.vu.nl/mailman/listinfo/icmns-beta). In the meantime, we look forward to seeing you at [ICMNS 2027 in Montpellier](https://project.inria.fr/icmns2027/), 1–4 June 2027.
