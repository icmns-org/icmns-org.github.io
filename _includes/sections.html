{%- comment -%} Lays out a page's content as numbered sections: each "## Heading" in the Markdown becomes a section with the heading on the left and its text on the right. Text before the first heading becomes the introduction. Usage: {% include sections.html html=content %} {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign parts = include.html | split: '{{ intro }} {%- endif %} {%- for part in parts offset: 1 %} {%- assign head = part | split: '' | first -%} {%- assign body = part | remove_first: head | remove_first: '' %}
{{ body }}
{%- endfor %}
