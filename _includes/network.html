{%- comment -%} The ICMNS network: nodes from _data/network.yml on a dotted world map (Equal Earth projection, made by tools/make_land_dots.py), linked like neurons: every node connects to its nearest neighbours, and a minimum spanning tree keeps the whole network connected. Spikes travel along the links; when one arrives, the node fires and may send spikes on. {%- endcomment -%}
